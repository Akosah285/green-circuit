# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

A balanced personal and professional audience visiting a personal portfolio.

## Product Purpose

Help visitors understand the author's work and interests, then explore further.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Builds and Schoolwork are separate destinations.
- Academics covers education and coursework; Journey covers personal chronology.
- Private Lab remains discoverable with an explicit private-access label.
- The website is still being built. Existing placeholders are intentional and must not be treated as completed claims or replaced with invented evidence.
- Improvements preserve the established identity rather than replacing it.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing profile, project, journey, and coursework content is centralized in `src\data\portfolio.ts`. Its presence is not confirmation that every entry or asset is final.

## Product Principles

- Introduce work and interests before inviting deeper exploration.
- Keep builds and school projects independently discoverable.
- Make private access and loading or failure states understandable.
- Preserve honest unfinished states until real content and artifacts are ready.

## Open Decisions

- Final project content, public artifacts, and featured selections.
- The exact access policy and public explanation for Private Lab.
- Future visual prototyping workflow.
